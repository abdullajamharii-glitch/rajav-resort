Room Guide

Rooms & Booking FAQ

Find answers to common questions regarding our suites, amenities, and coastal location.

Does Cherai beach resort have an infinity pool?

▼

Absolutely. Our beachfront infinity pool is located right next to the shore, offering stunning views of the Arabian Sea. Every single guest—whether you've booked our signature jacuzzi suite or our cozy economy room—enjoys full, free access to the pool deck and comfortable lounge chairs.

Can I book the entire Rajav resort for a private event?

▼

Yes, we do. For ₹50,000 per night, you can rent the entire property exclusively for your group. This includes private access to all 5 rooms, our beachfront infinity pool, and direct beach entry, complete with our dedicated culinary and concierge team.

Is breakfast included in the room rates?

▼

Complimentary breakfast is included when you stay in our Premium Jacuzzi room, Premium Beach View room, or book the entire resort. If you stay in our Balcony or Economy rooms, you can easily add breakfast to your booking or order fresh meals at our beachside cafe.

Does the resort provide secure parking facilities?

▼

Yes, we provide free, secure parking inside our gated property. Your vehicle will be completely safe with us during your stay.

What is the distance from Kochi Airport to Cherai?

▼

We are about 27 km away from Cochin International Airport (COK), which is usually a 45 to 60-minute drive. If you need a ride, our team is happy to arrange airport pickups and drop-offs for you.
