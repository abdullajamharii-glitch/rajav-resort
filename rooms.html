Room Guide

Rooms & Booking FAQ

Find answers to common questions regarding our suites, amenities, and coastal location.

Does Cherai beach resort have an infinity pool?

▼

Yes! Rajav Resort has a beautiful beachfront infinity pool that directly overlooks the Arabian Sea. All guests, regardless of room tier (including our Economy rooms), have full complimentary access to the pool deck and sunbeds.

Can I book the entire Rajav resort for a private event?

▼

Yes, we offer an exclusive Entire Resort Booking package. You can rent the entire property for ₹50,000 per night. This includes all 5 guest suites, private pool access, beach frontage, and a dedicated services team for corporate gatherings, weddings, or family getaways.

Is breakfast included in the room rates?

▼

Complimentary breakfast is included with our Premium Jacuzzi Bathtub Room, Premium Beach View Room, and Entire Resort Booking. For our Balcony and Economy rooms, you can add breakfast during your booking, or enjoy customized meals at our Beachside Café.

Does the resort provide secure parking facilities?

▼

Yes, Rajav Resort provides secure, gated, on-site parking for all our guests. Parking is completely free during your stay, whether you are staying in our suites or booking the entire property.

What is the distance from Kochi Airport to Cherai?

▼

Rajav Resort is located approximately 27 km (within 30 km) from the Cochin International Airport (COK). The driving time is roughly 45 to 60 minutes depending on traffic. We can arrange airport pickups and drop-offs upon request.
